wailing_caverns_instance.blp and wailing_caverns_route.blp: Dungeon Maps navigation
reconstruction from Blizzard's Wailing Caverns minimap textures. The four approach-chamber
textures are excluded. Geometry remains in the client's coordinate frame. Artwork/geometry
derived from Blizzard's game assets remains Blizzard Entertainment's; the reconstruction
script is tools/build_wailing_map.py. Placement data: Forever Dungeon Routes (MIT).
Turtle WoW's Atlas maps were reviewed as references; Turtle-only dungeon rooms/bosses were
not imported into the Forever map.
